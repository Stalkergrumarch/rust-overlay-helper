## 🧩 About

**Rust Overlay Helper** is a comprehensive mod menu for Rust featuring ESP, aim assistance, resource radar, crafting tracker, stash alert, and no recoil.

Based on popular tools like **RustCheat**, **RustAim**, and **RustRadar**.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- ESP — see players, loot, and resources through walls
- Aim Assist — smooth aimbot with adjustable FOV
- No Recoil — eliminate weapon recoil
- Resource Radar — locate nodes, sulfur, and hemp
- Stash Alert — notify when stashes are opened

### 🎨 Visuals & Overlay
- Player Boxes — display names and health
- Distance Indicators — show distance to entities
- Chams — colored player models
- Crosshair Overlay — custom crosshair options

### 🏋️ Utility & Training
- Crafting Tracker — real-time recipe calculator
- Instant Craft — skip crafting time
- No Fall Damage — survive any fall
- Speed Hack — adjust movement speed
- Unlimited Jump — bypass jump stamina
